My name is Johan Borgström, and I am a Designer & Technical Artist. Petfactory was founded with the goal of fusing **Design** and **Animation**. When coding, I prefer to work with Python and VEX.


I studied industrial design at [Umeå Institute of Design](https://www.umu.se/en/umea-institute-of-design/){: target="_blank" }, where I earned a BFA in Industrial Design. For the first year of my master’s degree, I continued at Umeå Institute of Design, focusing on interaction design. In the second year, I studied at [HDK-Valand Academy of Art and Design](https://www.gu.se/en/hdk-valand){: target="_blank" }, graduating with an MFA in Design.


Feel free to contact me: johan [ at ] petfactory.se
